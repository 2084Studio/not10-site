The Book

How did a revolutionlike this become possible?

Not Ten Years is an attempt to rethink how the Cultural Revolution became possible.

Rather than treating it simply as a sequence of political events, or reducing it to a struggle among a small circle of leaders, the book asks a more fundamental question.

The story begins before 1966 — in the political structures and habits formed through earlier campaigns, the transformation of language into a form of power, and the erosion of rules and institutional boundaries.

What the book asks

Not only who launched the Cultural Revolution, or who suffered under it.

  1. 言

    Language

    How political power entered language — and how language divided people into new categories.

  2. 革

    Rules

    How rules and institutional boundaries eroded, until institutions could no longer protect those inside them.

  3. 名

    Labels

    How labels, accusations, mass mobilization and loyalty could reshape an individual life.

  4. 人

    Survival

    How ordinary people tried to survive in a world where the rules themselves could suddenly change.
